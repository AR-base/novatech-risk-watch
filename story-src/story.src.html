Contain it

What happens on the team's phones when a cyberattack is spotted, from the first alert to the incident closed.

  1. An indicator crosses its threshold

    The dashboard alerts the people responsible on their phones before the risk turns into an incident.

  2. The CEO approves

    Containing can stop services and lock laptops, so the request goes to the CEO’s phone with what is at stake.

  3. Everyone gets their task

    Each step of the playbook goes to the person who owns it. Their Done button ticks it on every screen.

  4. The incident is closed

    For a cyberattack the demo laptop isolates itself; when every step is done the loss avoided is secured.
